// The science behind the sprint

Built around how your brain actually works.

01

Executive dysfunction is real

ADHD brains don't lack ideas or capability. They struggle with task initiation. The sprint's daily structure creates the external scaffold your prefrontal cortex needs to get past the blank page.

02

Body doubling works

Research shows people with ADHD are significantly more productive working alongside others, even virtually. The Triad puts three founders on the same deadline, competing together. Accountability built in.

03

Time blindness needs urgency

ADHD brains struggle with future time perception. "Someday" doesn't activate anything. A hard 72-hour window with a live leaderboard creates the now-or-never urgency that switches the brain on.

04

Dopamine drives completion

The ADHD brain runs on high-stimulation, deadline-driven bursts. The sprint is built for exactly one weekend of focused work. Not a 12-week program you'll drop by week two.
